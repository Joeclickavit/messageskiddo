How it works

1. Private Messaging

Click ✎ and enter someone's ID. Once you message them, they appear in your list.

2. Nicknames & Contacts

In any chat, click ⓘ to save a nickname. People must have a nickname to be selectable for Groups.

3. Group Chats

Click 👥, select members from your saved contacts, and name the group. Every person must already have a saved nickname before they can be added. To add someone new, start a chat with their ID, open ⓘ, enter a nickname, and save it first.

4. Group Roles, Kicks & AutoMod

The group owner can promote members to Admin. Owners and Admins can remove members from a group. Open ⓘ in a group and use AutoMod to block links, words, or phrases; each rule goes on its own line.

5. Unread & Seen

Unread message counts appear beside conversations. Open a conversation to mark it read. In private chats, a Read label appears when the other person has read your message; group messages show Seen when all other group members have read them. The browser tab title shows your total unread count, like (3) Messages. Chats are sorted with the most recent conversation on top, and the chat list and open conversation refresh automatically every 10 seconds.

6. Back Up & Restore Contacts

Under your ID in the sidebar are two small buttons. Download Contacts asks you to choose a password, then saves an encrypted file with your contact nicknames and your chat ID. Nobody can read or edit the file without the password, and a file that has been changed will refuse to open. Restore Contacts lets you pick that file and enter the password: the nicknames are merged into the contacts you already have (if an ID is already saved, the backup's nickname replaces it), and each restored person is added back to your list so you can use them in groups right away. If the backup was made with a different chat ID than the one you have now, you'll be asked whether to switch to it. Choose OK to get your old chats back, or Cancel to keep your current ID. A forgotten password can't be recovered, so keep it somewhere safe along with the file.

7. Database Storage

If a message write fails because the database is full, the app attempts to clear older chat messages and retry the new message. Keep contact backups separately; this cleanup can remove chat history.

8. Group Contacts

In a group, tap a sender's ID above their bubble to add them to your contacts. Save a nickname before trying to add them to another group.

9. GIFs

Click the GIF button next to the message box to open the GIF picker. Browse the folders or type in the search box, then click a GIF to send it. Your 10 most recently sent GIFs appear in a Recent folder. Click the star in the corner of any GIF to favorite it; favorites collect in a Favorites folder (click the star again to remove one). GIFs are loaded from two libraries and merged: the main one plus the extra one (Joeclickavit/gifsextra, folders only). The app asks GitHub's API what is in a public GitHub repository (and remembers the list for a day) and delivers the files through jsDelivr, so a sent GIF is just a small link. If GitHub's free rate limit is reached, wait a while and try again.

10. Voice Messages

Click the microphone next to the message box and allow microphone access if your browser asks. A red recording bar replaces the textbox: click the blue arrow to send, or the × (or press Esc) to cancel. The green bar next to the timer moves while the microphone hears you, so you can tell right away if it isn't working, and you'll be warned before sending a recording with no sound. Recordings stop automatically at 1 minute. A voice message shows up as a small player: click the play button to listen, and click it again to pause. Voice messages need a browser that can record audio, and a secure page (https or localhost).

11. Emojis

Click the smiley next to the message box to open the emoji picker. Browse by category or search, and click an emoji to add it to your message. You can also type a colon and the start of a name, like :smi, and pick from the suggestions with the arrow keys and Enter (or Tab), or by clicking. Emojis are sent as :name: text and appear as small animated pictures inside the message. A message made of only emojis is shown bigger. The emoji list comes straight from the files in the GitHub repository, so added or removed emojis show up on their own within about half an hour; the ↻ button in the picker reloads it right away. If the emoji list can't load, :name: just shows as plain text.

12. Links

Links (http, https or www) in messages are clickable. When you click one, you are asked whether to open it in a new tab.

13. Blocking

Click the block icon in the sidebar to block a contact ID or unblock people. You can also use Block User in a chat's details, or Block next to a group member. Blocked people disappear from your list and their messages are hidden.

14. Deleting Contacts and Groups

Delete Contact (in a private chat's details) removes the nickname and chat from your list. Group owners get Delete Group Forever, which removes the group and its messages for everyone.

15. Live AutoMod

AutoMod rules save automatically as you type and update live for every member.

16. Replies, Reactions, Edit & Unsend

Hover a message to see its tools: reply, react, pin, edit (your own text messages) and unsend (your own messages). Replies show the original above your message; click the quote to jump to it. Reactions appear under the message; click one to add or remove yours.

17. Typing, Search, Pins & Mute

"is typing..." shows while someone writes. The magnifier in the chat header searches that conversation. Pin a message from its tools (in groups only the owner and Admins can) and it stays in a bar at the top. In a chat's details you can pin the chat to the top of your list or mute its notifications (muted chats do not count in the tab title).

18. Mentions

In a group, type @ and pick a member (or @everyone). Mentioned people see a yellow outline around the message. Names with spaces use underscores.

19. Invite Codes

Owners and Admins can generate a code in a group's details. To join, click the pencil, enter the code under "Group invite code" and press Join. You are added as soon as an owner or Admin is online. Generate a new code or revoke it any time.

20. Mod Tools

Owners and Admins can Mute a member for a number of minutes (they cannot send while muted) and see the AutoMod Log of blocked messages in the group details.

21. Calls & Webcam

Click the phone icon in any chat header to start a voice call, in private chats and groups alike. The other person (or everyone in the group) sees a ringing banner and a 📞 next to the chat; click the green button to join. Inside a call use the buttons at the bottom to mute your microphone, turn your webcam on or off, share your screen, or leave. People with their camera off show as a letter circle, and a green outline appears around whoever is talking. While in a call you can go back to your chats and use Open call or Leave at the bottom of the sidebar. Calls connect directly between browsers, so they work best with up to about 8 people. Your browser asks for microphone and camera permission, and this needs https or localhost.
